Who We Are

Shenzhen Zhidi Technology Co., Ltd.

Founded as a manufacturer and trading company in 2019, ZHIDI Tech specializes in the R&D and production of industrial mixing, grinding, and processing equipment. With our own factory located in Huizhou, Guangdong, we control every step from design to delivery — ensuring consistent quality and competitive pricing.

Our core product line — the Planetary Vacuum Stirring Defoaming Machine — serves industries ranging from lithium battery manufacturing to electronic ceramics, coatings, adhesives, and cosmetics. From 1L lab-scale to 200L+ production models, we provide one-stop equipment solutions for your entire growth journey.

As a 1-year Gold Supplier on Alibaba.com with on-site factory verification and Trade Assurance, our international customers can order with confidence — backed by third-party auditing and payment protection.
