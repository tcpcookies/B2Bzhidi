Planetary Centrifugal Vacuum Mixer

Our flagship product — 36 models from 500ml R&D scale to 50L+ mass production

The ZHIDI Planetary Centrifugal Vacuum Mixer combines high-speed planetary rotation with vacuum defoaming technology. The dual-axis design generates intense shear forces while the vacuum chamber removes air bubbles — producing homogeneous, bubble-free mixtures with every batch.

Available from 500ml lab-scale to 50L+ production-scale. Pricing from $5,660 to $60,000. Configurable for lithium battery slurry, ceramic pastes, epoxy resins, LED phosphor, and adhesive compounds. Temperature control and multi-step programming options available on select models.
