Planetary Centrifugal Vacuum Mixer

Our flagship product line — 36 models from 500ml lab-scale to 50L+ production

The ZHIDI Planetary Centrifugal Vacuum Mixer combines high-speed planetary rotation with vacuum defoaming technology. The dual-axis design generates intense shear forces while the vacuum chamber removes air bubbles — producing homogeneous, bubble-free mixtures every time.

Available from 500ml lab-scale to 50L+ production-scale with pricing from $5,660 to $60,000. Configurable for lithium battery slurry, ceramic pastes, epoxy resins, LED phosphor, and adhesive compounds.
