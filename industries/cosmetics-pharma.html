Challenges & Solutions

Key Challenges in Cosmetics & Pharma Mixing

GMP Compliance

Pharmaceutical production requires validated equipment with full documentation. We provide IQ/OQ protocols, material certificates, and validation support.

Cleanability

Cross-contamination is unacceptable in pharma and cosmetics. CIP (Clean-In-Place) design with smooth surfaces and no dead zones enables thorough cleaning.

Deaeration

Air bubbles affect product appearance and dosing accuracy. Vacuum mixing removes entrapped air for smooth, bubble-free creams and ointments.

Temperature Control

Heat-sensitive APIs and active ingredients require precise temperature management. Jacketed vessels with circulation systems maintain exact temperatures.
