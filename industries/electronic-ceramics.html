Electronic Ceramics

MLCC and LTCC manufacturing requires ultra-fine ceramic slurry with precise viscosity control. Our ceramic-specific planetary mixers prevent sedimentation and ensure uniform particle distribution — essential for achieving consistent dielectric properties and layer thickness in multilayer ceramic components.

From dielectric slurry preparation to conductive paste blending, ZHIDI's mixing and grinding solutions cover every stage of electronic ceramics production. Our ceramic model mixers feature specialized scraper designs and temperature control for optimal processing results.

Challenges & Solutions

Key Challenges in Electronic Ceramics Mixing

Anti-Sedimentation

Heavy ceramic particles settle quickly in low-viscosity slurry. Continuous planetary agitation keeps particles in uniform suspension throughout the mixing cycle.

Viscosity Precision

MLCC and LTCC processes require slurry within tight viscosity ranges. Our mixers feature precise speed control and temperature management for consistent results.

Particle Distribution

Sub-micron ceramic powders tend to agglomerate. Combined planetary mixing and scraping grinding breaks down agglomerates for uniform nano-level distribution.

Zero Contamination

Even trace metallic impurities degrade dielectric performance. SUS316L contact surfaces and wear-resistant ceramic-coated agitators prevent contamination.
