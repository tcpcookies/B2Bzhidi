Industry Solutions

Coatings & Inks

High-viscosity coatings and UV inks require precise grinding and dispersion. Our three-roll grinding machines achieve uniform particle size and color consistency — the foundation of every high-quality coating and ink product.

From UV-curable inks that demand sub-micron pigment dispersion to industrial coatings requiring perfect color matching across batches, ZHIDI's grinding and mixing equipment delivers reliable, repeatable results for coatings manufacturers worldwide.

Challenges & Solutions

Key Challenges in Coatings & Inks Production

Color Consistency

Uneven pigment dispersion causes color variation between batches. Our three-roll grinders achieve uniform particle fineness for consistent color matching every time.

Particle Fineness

High-performance coatings require sub-micron particle size. Precision gap control on our grinding machines delivers consistent fineness down to 1-5 microns.

High-Viscosity Handling

Thick pastes and high-solid coatings resist conventional mixing. Our planetary mixers generate intense shear forces to disperse even the most viscous formulations.
