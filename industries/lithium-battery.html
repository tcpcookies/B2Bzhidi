Industry Solutions

Lithium Battery & New Energy

Lithium battery electrode slurry mixing requires high-precision planetary mixing under vacuum. Our machines ensure uniform slurry with no bubbles — critical for battery performance. The consistency of electrode slurry directly impacts energy density, cycle life, and safety of lithium-ion cells.

Whether you are producing cathode slurry (NCM, LFP, LCO), anode slurry (graphite, silicon-carbon), electrolyte formulations, or developing next-generation solid-state batteries, ZHIDI's planetary vacuum mixers deliver the homogeneous, bubble-free results your process demands.

Challenges & Solutions

Key Challenges in Lithium Battery Mixing

Bubble Removal

Entrapped air in electrode slurry causes coating defects and reduces battery performance. Our vacuum system achieves ≤ -0.095 MPa for complete defoaming.

Uniform Dispersion

Active materials, conductive agents, and binders must be uniformly distributed. Double planetary rotation ensures thorough kneading and shear mixing.

Viscosity Control

High-viscosity slurry (up to 50,000 mPa·s) requires powerful agitation. Our mixers handle extreme viscosities with consistent results batch after batch.

Material Purity

SUS316L contact surfaces prevent metallic contamination. Sealed vacuum operation protects moisture-sensitive materials like electrolyte components.
