Adhesives & Sealants

Epoxy, silicone, and thermal grease compounding requires bubble-free mixing under vacuum. Our planetary mixers handle viscosities up to 1,000,000 mPa·s — delivering homogeneous, air-free products that meet the strict performance requirements of electronics, automotive, and construction applications.

Whether you are producing room-temperature vulcanizing silicone sealants, two-component epoxy adhesives, or thermally conductive greases for LED and semiconductor packaging, ZHIDI's vacuum mixing technology ensures product consistency and eliminates the defects caused by entrapped air.

Challenges & Solutions

Key Challenges in Adhesives & Sealants Mixing

Bubble Elimination

Air bubbles weaken adhesive bonds and reduce thermal conductivity. Vacuum mixing at ≤ -0.095 MPa removes all entrapped air for defect-free products.

Extreme Viscosity

Silicone sealants and thermal greases can exceed 500,000 mPa·s. Our double planetary design generates sufficient shear to mix these ultra-high-viscosity materials uniformly.

Filler Dispersion

Thermal conductive fillers (alumina, boron nitride) must be evenly distributed. Planetary rotation ensures uniform filler dispersion without dead zones.

Wall Scraping

High-viscosity materials cling to vessel walls. Our wall-mounted scrapers continuously push material back into the mixing zone, ensuring 100% product homogeneity.
