Industries We Serve

Our equipment powers innovation across 6 core manufacturing sectors worldwide

Lithium Battery

Electrode slurry mixing, electrolyte preparation, and separator coating. Our vacuum mixers deliver the homogeneity and degassing precision required for EV, consumer electronics, and energy storage cells.

Electronic Ceramics

MLCC paste mixing, ceramic powder dispersion, and substrate coating. Ceramic chamber options available for metal-free processing — critical for semiconductor-grade materials.

Coatings & Inks

Pigment dispersion, ink homogenization, and coating formulation. Our mixers handle a wide viscosity range — from water-based inks to heavy UV-curable coatings.

Adhesives & Sealants

Silicone sealants, epoxy adhesives, polyurethane, and solder paste. Heavy-duty double planetary mixers handle extreme viscosities for construction and electronics assembly.

Cosmetics & Pharma

Creams, lotions, ointments, and API compounding. Lab-scale to production-scale equipment with stainless steel chambers and cGMP-compatible processing.

Lab & Research

Planetary ball mills and small-batch vacuum mixers for materials science, nanomaterial synthesis, and pharmaceutical R&D. Compact, programmable, and reproducible.
