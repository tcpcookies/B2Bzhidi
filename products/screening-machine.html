Working Principle

How It Works

1. Load Material

Pour unsorted mealworm culture — containing worms of all sizes, pupae, beetles, dead worms, and feces — into the screening machine hopper. The multi-layer screen system handles the entire mix in one pass.

2. Multi-Stage Separation

The screening system separates by size through multiple mesh layers: feces and dead worms fall through first, then small worms, medium worms, and large worms are each sorted into their own collection area. Pupae and beetles are separated simultaneously.

3. Collect & Process

Graded mealworms are collected through individual discharge outlets — ready for sale, further processing, or return to breeding trays. The dust-free design ensures clean, hygienic output suitable for food and feed applications.
