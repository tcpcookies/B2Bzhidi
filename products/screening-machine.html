Working Principle

How It Works

1. Feed Material

Raw material is fed into the top of the screening machine. The motor vibration drive generates high-frequency, low-amplitude vibrations that distribute material evenly across the screen surface.

2. Multi-Stage Classification

Material passes through multiple mesh layers of decreasing aperture size. Each layer captures a specific particle size range, enabling simultaneous classification into up to 5 size fractions.

3. Collect & Clean

Classified materials are discharged through individual outlets. The dust-proof sealed design prevents cross-contamination, and the easy-clean construction allows quick changeover between batches.
