Working Principle

How It Works

1. Load & Position

Place screen frames horizontally on the drying racks. The horizontal orientation ensures even exposure to airflow and prevents emulsion sagging or frame warping during drying.

2. Precision Drying

Multi-zone heating with digital temperature control maintains uniform heat distribution (±2°C) across all frames. The timer function ensures consistent drying cycles every time.

3. Cool & Remove

After the drying cycle completes, the automatic cool-down phase prevents thermal shock. Remove fully cured frames ready for the next production step.
