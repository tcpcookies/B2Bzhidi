Working Principle

How It Works

1. Planetary Motion

Grinding jars rotate on their own axis while simultaneously orbiting the central axis — creating extreme centrifugal forces that accelerate grinding balls to pulverize material to nanometer scale.

2. Programmable Control

Set rotation speed, grinding duration, pause intervals, and reverse rotation cycles via the digital control panel. Program multiple steps for complex grinding protocols.

3. Collect & Analyze

After the programmed cycle, remove grinding jars and collect the finely milled powder. Suitable for wet or dry grinding with optional vacuum jar lids for oxygen-sensitive materials.
