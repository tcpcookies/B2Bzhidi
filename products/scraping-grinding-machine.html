Automatic Scraping & Grinding Machine

The ZHIDI Automatic Scraping & Grinding Machine is a high-precision three-roll grinding machine designed for the dispersion and fine grinding of pigments, inks, coatings, and pastes. The three precision-ground SUS304 rollers operate at different speeds, generating intense shear forces that break down agglomerates to achieve uniform particle size distribution. Automated gap adjustment ensures consistent quality and reduces operator dependency across lab and production scales.

Working Principle

How It Works

1. Three-Roll Shearing

Material passes through the gap between three counter-rotating rollers at increasing speeds, generating intense shear forces that break down agglomerates and disperse particles uniformly.

2. Automated Gap Control

Servo-driven gap adjustment maintains precise roller spacing, ensuring consistent particle fineness and repeatability across batches without manual intervention.

3. Cool & Collect

Water cooling circulation prevents overheating during grinding. The finely processed material is collected by the scraper blade on the final roller for immediate use or packaging.
