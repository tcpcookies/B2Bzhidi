Working Principle

How It Works

1. Agitator Selection

Choose the optimal agitator type — anchor for high-viscosity, paddle for medium, turbine for low-viscosity, or high-shear for emulsification — based on your material characteristics.

2. Variable Speed Mixing

PLC-controlled variable speed drive adjusts agitation intensity in real time. Jacketed vessel option enables simultaneous heating or cooling during the mixing process.

3. Discharge & CIP

Bottom valve or pressurized discharge for easy material removal. CIP system cleans the vessel and agitator in place, minimizing downtime between batch changeovers.
