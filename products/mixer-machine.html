Working Principle

How It Works

1. Dispersion Mixing

Variable-frequency speed control enables precise dispersion of ink pigments and color modulation. The high-shear impeller breaks down agglomerates for uniform color development in the mixing drum.

2. Barrel Rolling

Paint rolling machines continuously tumble 20-40kg barrels to prevent sedimentation, maintain uniform consistency, and accelerate curing. Node-type rolling for anti-sedimentation or aging roller for paint maturation.

3. Quality Output

The result is consistent, sediment-free ink or paint ready for production. Electric frequency conversion ensures reproducible results batch after batch. Customized specifications available for specialized formulations.
