How It Works

Dual-Axis Planetary Mixing with Vacuum

Three stages to achieve perfectly uniform, bubble-free mixtures

1. Planetary Rotation

Dual-axis rotation creates intense shear forces. The material container rotates on its own axis while orbiting the central axis — ensuring 360° material turnover with zero dead zones.

2. Vacuum Defoaming

Built-in vacuum pump evacuates the chamber to remove entrained air. Achieves residual bubble content below 0.1% — critical for battery slurry and optical-grade applications.

3. Controlled Discharge

Programmable multi-step cycles with temperature monitoring. Automatic stop at completion. Homogeneous, degassed material ready for the next production stage.
