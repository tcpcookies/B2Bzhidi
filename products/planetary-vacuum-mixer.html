Planetary Vacuum Stirring Defoaming Machine

The ZHIDI Planetary Vacuum Stirring Defoaming Machine integrates double planetary rotation with vacuum defoaming in a single, compact unit. The dual-axis design — where the planetary frame rotates while individual agitators spin on their own axes — generates intense shear and kneading forces. Simultaneously, the vacuum chamber removes entrapped air bubbles, producing homogeneous, bubble-free mixtures ideal for high-performance applications.

Working Principle

How It Works

1. Double Planetary Rotation

The planetary frame rotates while agitators spin on their own axes, creating strong shear and kneading forces that thoroughly mix high-viscosity materials.

2. Vacuum Defoaming

The sealed vacuum chamber (≤ -0.095 MPa) removes air bubbles and volatile gases from the mixture, ensuring a bubble-free, homogeneous final product.

3. Discharge & Clean

Pressurized discharge or bottom valve for easy material removal. CIP (Clean-In-Place) design available for quick changeover between batches.
