Double Planetary Mixer

The ZHIDI Double Planetary Mixer uses dual planetary rotation to generate intense shear and kneading forces for thorough mixing of high-viscosity materials. Available from 1L lab-scale to 100L production models with optional vacuum, heating, and integrated pressing.
