Planetary Vacuum Stirring Defoaming Machine

Our flagship product line — double planetary mixing with integrated vacuum defoaming

Available from 15L to 100L+ production-scale units with real-world pricing starting at CNY 84,175, with industry-specific configurations for lithium battery slurry, ceramic pastes, epoxy resins, silicone, and adhesive compounds. Optional heating and temperature control.
