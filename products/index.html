Planetary Vacuum Stirring Defoaming Machine

Our flagship product line — double planetary mixing with integrated vacuum defoaming

The ZHIDI Planetary Vacuum Stirring Defoaming Machine combines double planetary rotation with vacuum defoaming in a single unit. The dual-axis design generates strong shear forces while the vacuum chamber removes air bubbles — producing homogeneous, bubble-free mixtures every time.

Available from 5L laboratory models to 200L+ production-scale units, with industry-specific configurations for lithium battery slurry, ceramic pastes, and adhesive compounds.
