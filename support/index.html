Frequently Asked

What is the typical lead time?
Standard lead time is 10 working days from order confirmation. Custom configurations may require an additional 3-5 days. We ship via sea freight (FOB/CIF) or air freight (express) based on your preference.
Do you offer OEM / private labeling?
Yes. We offer OEM manufacturing, design services, and buyer label options. Minimum order quantities apply for customized branding. Contact our sales team for details.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept T/T (wire transfer), PayPal, and cash. Payment currencies include USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CNY, and HKD. Trade Assurance is available on Alibaba.com.
Can I visit the factory before ordering?
Absolutely. We welcome factory audits and facility tours. If travel is not possible, we can arrange a live video walkthrough of our production floor. Contact us to schedule.
