Technical

Contact our engineering team with your: (1) material type and viscosity, (2) batch size, (3) application/industry, (4) special requirements. We'll recommend the most suitable model and configuration.

Planetary mixing uses dual-axis rotation (planetary frame + individual agitators) creating strong shear and kneading forces. This is far more effective for high-viscosity materials (10,000+ mPa·s) compared to single-axis mixing. Combined with vacuum, it produces bubble-free results.
