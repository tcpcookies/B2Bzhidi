Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our products, ordering, shipping, and support

What is the typical lead time?
Standard lead time is 10 working days from order confirmation. Custom configurations may require 3-5 additional days. Rush production available for urgent orders.
Do you offer OEM / private labeling?
Yes. We offer OEM manufacturing, design customization, and buyer label options. MOQ applies for branded customization. Contact our sales team for details.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept T/T (wire transfer), PayPal, and cash. Currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CNY, HKD. Trade Assurance available on Alibaba.com for payment protection.
What shipping terms are available?
FOB (Yantian Port), CIF, EXW, and DEQ. We work with major freight forwarders and can ship to your designated port or door-to-door. Express air freight available for small equipment.
Can I visit your factory?
Absolutely. We welcome factory audits at our Huizhou, Guangdong facility. For customers unable to travel, we offer live video factory tours. Schedule through our contact page.
What certifications do your machines have?
Our equipment carries CE and ISO 9001 certifications. Additional certifications (UL, CSA) available upon request for specific markets.
What is the warranty period?
All equipment comes with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering parts and workmanship. Extended warranty plans (2-3 years) available. Spare parts shipped within 5 working days.
Do you provide installation and training?
Video installation guides and remote support are included with every purchase. On-site installation and operator training by our engineers is available for orders above $30,000 or upon request.
What is your minimum order quantity?
MOQ is 1 unit for most standard models. Sample orders welcome — especially for our 500ml and 1L lab-scale models. Custom configurations may have higher MOQ requirements.
How do I choose the right mixer for my application?
Contact our engineering team with your material type, batch size, viscosity, and any special requirements (vacuum, temperature control, material compatibility). We'll recommend the optimal configuration within 24 hours.

Still have questions?

Our team is ready to help. WhatsApp response within 1 hour.
